Shipping Information

Everything you need to know about how and when your order will arrive.

Made With Intention

Each SAVIG STYLE T-shirt is made especially for you as soon as you place an order, rather than kept in bulk inventory. Making products on demand helps reduce overproduction — which is why it takes us a little longer to get your order out the door, but we think it's worth it.

Delivery Times

  • Standard delivery: 3–5 business days
  • Express delivery: 1–2 business days, available for an additional fee
  • International orders: tracked shipping is available to 50+ countries

Shipping Cost

Buy 5 or more products in a single order and enjoy free standard shipping. For smaller orders, shipping charges (and any express delivery fee) will be confirmed with you directly over WhatsApp before you send payment.

How Orders Are Placed & Tracked

Since orders are confirmed over WhatsApp, our team will keep you updated on your order status, dispatch, and delivery timeline directly in that same conversation.

Questions?

If you have any questions about your shipment, reach out any time via our contact page or message us directly on WhatsApp using the button in the corner of the screen.
