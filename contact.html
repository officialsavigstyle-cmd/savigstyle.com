FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the US. Express shipping (1-2 business days) is available for an additional $12. International orders typically take 7-14 business days.

What's your return policy?

We offer a 60-day hassle-free return policy. If you're not satisfied, simply return the item in its original condition for a full refund or exchange. Free return shipping on domestic orders.

How should I care for my SAVIG STYLE tee?

Machine wash on a gentle cycle at 30°C with like colors. Lay flat to dry away from direct sunlight. Do not tumble dry. Iron on low heat if needed. Our premium cotton actually gets softer with each wash.

Do you offer wholesale or bulk orders?

Yes! We offer wholesale pricing for orders of 50+ pieces. Please contact us at wholesale@savigstyle.com with your requirements, and we'll provide a custom quote.

Are your products ethically made?

Absolutely. All our products are made in GOTS-certified workshops where workers are paid above living wage. We use 100% organic cotton grown without synthetic pesticides, and our dye processes are environmentally responsible.
