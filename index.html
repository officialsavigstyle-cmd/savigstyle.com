Got Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add the T-Shirts you want to your cart, choose your size and quantity, then check out through our cart page you'll be able to send your order details straight to us on WhatsApp to confirm payment and delivery. You can also message us directly on WhatsApp from any product page.

What sizes do you offer?

Our T-Shirts come in S, M, L, XL, and XXL. Every product page includes a full size chart with chest, length, shoulder, and sleeve measurements to help you pick the right fit.

How long does shipping take?

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Need it sooner? Express delivery is available in 1-2 business days for an additional fee. We also offer tracked shipping to 50+ countries for international orders.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 60-day return policy. If you're not completely satisfied, return your item in its original condition for a full refund or exchange. See our Return Policy for full details.

What payment methods do you accept?

Once you confirm your order details on WhatsApp, our team will share the available payment options and delivery details with you directly.

What are your T-Shirts made of?

Every T-Shirt is made from 100% soft, lightweight cotton (150-160 GSM), pre-shrunk and side-seamed for a comfortable, durable everyday fit.
