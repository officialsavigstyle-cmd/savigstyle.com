Privacy Policy

How SAVIG STYLE collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to share with us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and message when you use our contact form, subscribe to our newsletter, or place an order with us over WhatsApp (including delivery address and order details).

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your order, and to communicate with you about it
  • To respond to questions or requests you send through our contact form or WhatsApp
  • To send newsletter updates, only if you've subscribed
  • To improve our products, website, and customer experience

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with the delivery partners needed to get your order to you, or when required by law.

WhatsApp Communication

Since orders and support are handled over WhatsApp, any details you share in that conversation (like your name, address, and order preferences) are used solely to fulfil and support your order.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time, and you're welcome to ask us to update or delete the personal information we hold about you by reaching out through our contact page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please get in touch via our contact page or message us directly on WhatsApp.
